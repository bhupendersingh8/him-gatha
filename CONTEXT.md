# HIM GATHA Digital Heritage Domain Context

HIM GATHA is the sacred digital atlas and living cultural archive of Himachal Pradesh, mapping village deities, traditional architecture, and cultural gatherings across all 12 districts.

## Language

### Sacred Entities & Worship
**Deity (Devta / Devi)**:
A localized sacred manifestation presiding over a specific village, pargana, or valley in Himachal Pradesh.
_Avoid_: God, idol, statue, pagan spirit.

**Mohra**:
An embossed brass, silver, or ashtadhatu face mask representing an ancestor deity or sacred presence mounted on a palanquin.
_Avoid_: Mask, faceplate, sculpture.

**Rath (Palanquin)**:
The wooden ceremonial chariot upon which the deity's Mohras and sacred garments are mounted to travel during fairs and pilgrimages.
_Avoid_: Cart, vehicle, carriage.

**Kardar**:
The hereditary administrative caretaker or traditional manager of a deity's temple, lands, and processions.
_Avoid_: Priest, guard, clerk.

**Gur (Oracle / Medium)**:
The initiated human conduit through whom a deity speaks, pronounces divine justice, or resolves community disputes.
_Avoid_: Shaman, witch doctor, fortune teller.

---

### Architectural & Vernacular Heritage
**Kath-Kuni**:
The ancient vernacular architecture of the Western Himalayas featuring alternating interlocked layers of heavy deodar timber beams and dry-stacked slate or stone masonry without mortar.
_Avoid_: Wood cabin, log house, stone hut.

**Dhaja (Finial / Shikhara)**:
The sacred carved pinnacle, kalasha, or conical canopy crowning a wooden temple tower.
_Avoid_: Spire, roof tip, pole.

**Kashtha-Kala**:
The intricate traditional wood-carving art adorning temple eaves, sanctum doors, and cantilevered balconies depicting folklore, flora, and geometric motifs.
_Avoid_: Woodcraft, carpentry.

---

### Festivals & Geography
**Dev-Mela**:
A sacred regional fair where village deities gather in their Raths for communal worship, cultural dances (Nati), and divine assemblies (such as Kullu Dussehra or Mandi Shivratri).
_Avoid_: Fair, carnival, festival party.

**Jatra / Shobhayatra**:
A ritual procession or pilgrimage in which a deity's Rath is carried across mountains, rivers, and neighboring villages.
_Avoid_: Parade, march.

**District (Zila)**:
One of the 12 administrative and cultural divisions of Himachal Pradesh (e.g., Chamba, Kullu, Mandi, Shimla, Kinnaur, Lahaul & Spiti, Kangra, Sirmaur, Solan, Bilaspur, Hamirpur, Una).
_Avoid_: State, province, zone.

Divine Administrative Framework

The traditional governance structure of local deities in Himachal Pradesh. Each role is crucial for managing the deity's affairs, properties, and divine processions.

Gur (Oracle / Shaman)

The human medium through whom the deity speaks. The Gur enters a divine trance to answer questions, resolve disputes, and predict the future for the local villagers.

Kardar (Manager)

The chief administrator of the deity's estate. They manage temple funds, organize festivals, and ensure that all daily rituals and grand processions run smoothly.

Pujari (Priest)

Responsible for conducting daily worship (Puja), lighting the sacred lamp, and offering food (Bhog) to the deity according to strict traditional and astrological timelines.

Bhandari (Storekeeper)

The guardian of the temple treasury (Bhandar). They safeguard the deity's gold, silver ornaments, traditional masks (Mohras), and essential grains collected from devotees.

Bajantris (Musicians)

Plays traditional instruments like the Dhol, Nagara, Karnal, and Narsingha to invoke the deity's presence and guide the rhythm of the palanquin during processions.

Chharidhar (Mace-bearer)

Leads processions carrying the sacred silver or wooden mace (Chhari). They maintain order among the crowd and symbolize the temporal and royal authority of the deity.

5. Dev-Khel & Oracle Records

Himachal mein "Dev-Khel" (Gurs ka khelna) ek atyant pavitra aur mahatvapurna parampara hai. Jab kisi khas devta ke "Gur" (Oracle) ke andar devta ka aavesh (possession) hota hai, toh woh kapne lagte hain aur dev-vaani (divine pronouncements) bolte hain.

Yeh khel aam taur par bade milon (jaise Kullu Dussehra ya Mandi Shivratri) ya gaon ke vishesh tyoharon par hota hai. Inhi Dev-Khels ke dauran badi bhavishyawaniyan ki jati hain, jinse kheti, mausam, aur aane wali vipdaon ke baare mein gaon walon ko aagah kiya jata hai. Is digital archive mein har devta ke Dev-Khel aur unki aitihasik bhavishyawaniyon ka record rakha gaya hai.

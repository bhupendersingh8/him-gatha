# Triage Label Vocabulary

The canonical labels used for triaging issues and tickets:
- `needs-triage`: Newly submitted tickets or issues requiring initial categorization.
- `needs-info`: Awaiting cultural verification or additional data from field researchers.
- `ready-for-agent`: Completely specified ticket ready for automated execution.
- `ready-for-human`: Requires manual human review or traditional administrative authorization.
- `wontfix`: Out of scope or invalid proposal.
